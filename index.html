About

A commerce background, now built into a research discipline.

Charitha is an MBA candidate in Finance and Business Analytics at Amity Chennai, building on a strong commerce foundation from her B.Com (General) degree.

Her interest in financial markets took practical shape during a structured internship at Xoduse Trading Academy, where she moved from reading financial statements to building valuation models, studying chart-based market structure, and tracking how global macroeconomic events feed into asset prices.

She approaches finance the way her academic training encourages her to — methodically: understand the fundamentals first, test them through valuation, then read the market's price behaviour and the macro backdrop before forming a view.

She is now working toward an entry-level role in financial analysis, business analysis, or equity research, where she can apply this analytical and strategic thinking in a professional setting.
